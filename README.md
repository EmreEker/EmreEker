### About

Senior Software Engineer with 10+ years of experience designing and building backend systems, with deep expertise in the Java/Spring ecosystem. My background spans payment and banking integrations, event-driven and distributed architectures, and — more recently — AI-powered backend systems built around retrieval-augmented generation and agentic tooling.

I care about building systems that are correct under failure — idempotent by design, observable, and resilient to the messy realities of production, especially in domains like payments where mistakes are costly.
